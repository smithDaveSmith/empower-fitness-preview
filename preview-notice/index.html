A concept.
For you.

This is an independent, unofficial redesign concept for Empower Fitness Hub. It is not operated by, endorsed by or connected to the business. Information was checked against its public website on 11 October 2026.

Bookings and contact

The consultation page can load the studio’s existing Calendly calendar when you choose to open it. Its direct link opens the same real booking destination. Email and telephone links contact the real business. This concept does not accept payments, create memberships or submit enquiry forms.

Your privacy in this preview

The preview sets no analytics or marketing cookies and collects no enquiry data. Its host may process access logs. Calendly and its providers may set cookies and process booking data after you open the calendar or follow its direct link. The calendar is not loaded before that action, and its own cookie controls remain available. Social platforms apply their own terms after you follow a link.

Photography and identity

Studio and portrait photography is reused from the official website for this concept. Copyright remains with the relevant owners. Reuse permission, client consent and current trainer information must be confirmed before an official launch. The eight-person diagram and typographic identity are proposed design elements.

Information to confirm before launch

Current timetable, personal training formats, membership prices, booking availability, photograph permissions and approved legal policies. The published timetable graphic appears to originate in 2023.
