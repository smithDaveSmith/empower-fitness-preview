More answers↗
Is this for beginners?

Yes. The team welcomes beginners and helps you choose a starting point.

How big are the groups?

Classes have a maximum of eight people and are led by a personal trainer.

Can I use the gym on my own?

The studio offers structured, coached sessions rather than independent open-gym access.

Can you adapt exercises around an injury?

Discuss it during your initial assessment. The team can review suitable exercise alternatives with you.

Make time.For you.

The first step is a conversation. Let’s find out what training could look like for you.
